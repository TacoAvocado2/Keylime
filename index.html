The Story Behind Chitter's Fritters

The story behind Chitter's Fritters begins with my Uncle Chitter. My grandmother raised eight children, five girls and three boys. My mother was the oldest and Uncle Chitter was the youngest. Because there were so many children, my mother helped care for her younger brothers and sisters from an early age.

Uncle Chitter was diagnosed with autism. He earned the nickname "Chitter" because he could talk and carry conversations before he was even two years old. Once he started talking, he never stopped. He loved conversations and would talk about anything and everything with anyone nearby.

There were also times when he would experience emotional outbursts. During those moments, my mother would head into the kitchen and fry a batch of my grandmother's famous Key Lime Ricotta Fritters.

The sound of the sizzling oil and the sweet aroma of key lime fritters would immediately bring a smile to Uncle Chitter's face. He knew something special was coming.

This family recipe has been passed down through multiple generations and remains at the heart of our business today.

After Uncle Chitter passed away due to heart complications, I decided to honor his memory by sharing our family's recipe with the world.

That is why the company is called Chitter's Fritters.

For every dozen fritters sold, $1 is donated to Autism Awareness in memory of Uncle Chitter.
