The Legacy

The Story Behind Chitter's Fritters

The story behind Chitter's Fritters begins with my Uncle Chitter, who was diagnosed with autism. He earned his name because he loved conversations and could talk with anyone about everything before he was even two years old.

During times when he would experience extreme emotional outbursts, my mother would step into the kitchen to fry a fresh batch of my grandmother's famous Key Lime Ricotta Fritters. The sizzle of the oil and the citrus aroma immediately brought a smile back to his face.

After Uncle Chitter passed away due to heart complications, I decided to honor his legacy by sharing this comforting recipe with families everywhere. That's why for every dozen fritters sold, $1 is donated directly to Autism Awareness initiatives.
