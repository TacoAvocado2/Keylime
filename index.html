OUR STORY

The Story Behind Chitter's Fritters

The story behind Chitter's Fritters begins with Uncle Chitter, whose love for conversation and family touched everyone around him.

Whenever life became difficult, the comforting aroma of freshly prepared Key Lime Fritters brought joy, connection, and a sense of home.

After Uncle Chitter passed away due to heart complications, his memory inspired the creation of Chitter's Fritters.

Today we honor that legacy through every batch we prepare, sharing a taste of Key West while supporting autism awareness and meaningful community initiatives.
